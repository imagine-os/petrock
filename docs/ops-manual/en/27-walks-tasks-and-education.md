# Walks, tasks and education

Three small screens that keep the floor and the team organised.

## 1. Walks (F-67)

Every outing is a row: pet, handler, start, minutes, potty yes / no, a note. Plan the day's walks in the morning; start and finish them from the list. Hotel guests get their included walks; daycare walks are a priced add-on billed later from these rows.

[screenshot: F-67 — Walk log]

> TIP: The note is for the parent as much as for us: "pulled on the leash, calm after ten minutes" is worth writing.

## 2. Tasks (F-68)

A task has a title, an assignee, a due date, a priority and a status. Two kinds: **task** (do once) and **checklist** (the opening and closing lists every day). Deleting a task needs a manager PIN; completing it does not.

[screenshot: F-68 — Tasks and checklists]

## 3. Education (F-66)

Every chapter of this manual is a lesson. Your progress shows per chapter: online read, in-person done, signed off by the manager. Managers see the whole team and sign off.

[screenshot: F-66 — Training progress]

## In-person lesson

1. Do one planned walk from the list: start it, walk, finish it with a note.
2. Run the opening checklist with the desk.

## Online lesson

1. Open **Walking (F-67)**; plan a walk for a demo pet, start it, finish it.
2. Open **Tasks (F-68)**; add a task for yourself, complete it; try to delete it and see the PIN modal.
3. Open **Education (F-66)** and find this chapter in your progress.
