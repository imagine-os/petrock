# Front desk daily operations

Your home screen is **Today (F-01)**: KPIs for the day, the reservations grouped as arriving / departing / staying / checked out, and every status change one click away. Everything you do is logged with your name.

[screenshot: F-01 — Today at the desk: tiles, day tabs, reservations table]

## 1. Opening the desk

1. Unlock, lights, HVAC; walk the rows once and check every guest is up and has water.
2. Sign in with your **PIN** (A-00). The top bar shows your location; it must be yours.
3. Open **Today** and read the four tabs in order: *Arriving* (who comes today, at what time), *Departing* (who leaves, balances due), *Staying*, *Checked out*.
4. Open **Notifications** (bell, F-60): vaccine proofs submitted overnight, new app bookings, customer messages. Mark them read as you handle them.
5. Print or note the arrivals list for the floor team and the walks planned for the day (F-67).

{{locations}}

> TIP: Arrivals and departures are computed from the booking dates and today's date (rule R-I05). If a booking looks wrong in a tab, the dates are wrong on the booking, not the tab.

## 2. The rhythm of the day

| When | Do |
| --- | --- |
| Hours before opening | Opening checklist (Tasks, F-68) |
| Morning | Check-ins (chapter 12), verify vaccine proofs (chapter 13), start walks |
| Midday | Daycare pickups and drop-offs (chapter 16), grooming hand-offs (chapter 15) |
| Afternoon | Check-outs and payments (chapters 12, 18), photos to parents |
| Closing | Closing checklist, meds logged, messages answered, drawer counted |

## 3. Status colours

Every reservation carries one of the lifecycle statuses. Learn the colours: they are the same on every screen, in the app and in this manual.

{{statuses}}

## 4. Closing

1. Every departure today must be *checked out*; every arrival *checked in* or explained in a note.
2. Answer or hand over open messages (F-61).
3. Complete the closing checklist in Tasks (F-68).
4. Leave feedback if something slowed you down (the Feedback button on every page writes straight to the owner).

> WARNING: Never change a status to *cancelled* or *no show* on your own: those moves ask for a manager PIN (chapter 17).

## In-person lesson

1. Shadow one full opening and one full closing with the manager.
2. Read the arrivals list aloud and say which room each guest goes to and why (size, shared room, penthouse fit).
3. Handle the bell notifications with the manager watching.

## Online lesson

1. Open **Today (F-01)** as the front desk demo user; switch the tabs; open a reservation.
2. Open **Notifications (F-60)**, filter by kind, mark one read and unread.
3. Open **Tasks (F-68)** and complete the opening checklist.
