# Manager duties

The manager runs one location: the desk and the floor follow the chapters, the manager makes the day work and holds the PIN. This chapter is the checklist the other chapters point to.

## 1. Every day

1. Opening walk with the desk (chapter 11); check the arrivals list against rooms and the timeline.
2. Clear the **vaccine verification queue** (F-52) with the desk before the first arrival.
3. Approve or refuse PIN requests as they come (chapter 17); never hand the PIN over.
4. Watch capacity: penthouse and suite rows, daycare yard, grooming slots.
5. Read the staff **Feedback** items for your location and answer or forward them (chapter 24).
6. Closing: statuses clean, balances collected, tasks done, incidents noted.

{{capacities}}

## 2. Every week

1. Staff schedule and working hours (Employees, F-55); statuses Active / Inactive / On leave.
2. Training: sign off in-person lessons in **Education (F-66)**; assign the next chapters to new hires.
3. Reviews: moderate what is pending (F-65).
4. Reports: read the location's week (chapter 23) and note anomalies for the owner.
5. Tasks: clean the board (F-68); delete stale tasks (needs your PIN).

[screenshot: F-66 — Education: training progress per employee]

## 3. Escalate to the owner

Pricing, discounts outside the rules, a new rule ("dogs over X must be a Suite"), a location setting, a refund larger than a night, a complaint about staff. The owner changes settings; you do not.

> NOTE: If you find yourself approving the same exception every week, it is a missing rule. Add it through **Settings › Rules** as *requested* (chapter 24).

## In-person lesson

1. Run one full day as acting manager with the owner reachable.
2. Sign off two in-person lessons in Education.

## Online lesson

1. Open **Education (F-66)**; sign off one completion.
2. Open **Reports (F-62)** and read the week for your location.
3. Open **Employees (F-55)** and set one employee to *On leave* and back.
