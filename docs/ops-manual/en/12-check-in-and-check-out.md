# Check-in and check-out

A check-in is the moment a reservation becomes a guest in a room. Everything that could go wrong later (wrong room, missing meds, unpaid balance, expired vaccine) is cheapest to catch here.

## 1. Before the parent arrives

1. Open the reservation from **Today › Arriving** (F-10).
2. Status must be **confirmed**. If it still says **pending vaccines**, the proof is not verified: go to chapter 13 first. Confirming without verified vaccines needs a manager PIN.
3. Check the room assignment: room type, shared room for multi-dog bookings, and the fit rules below.
4. Read the medical questionnaire: medication, dosing, flea treatment, medical alert.

## 2. The check-in conversation

1. Greet the parent and the dog by name.
2. Confirm dates and pick-up time; confirm who is allowed to pick up.
3. Take belongings (food, meds, toy) and label them with the room code.
4. Confirm meds and feeding in the booking notes; anything the parent adds goes into the booking, not on paper.
5. Take the deposit or balance if it is due (chapter 18).
6. Press **Check in**. No PIN is needed for check-in.

## 3. Check-out

1. From **Today › Departing** open the booking; the balance shows total minus deposits.
2. Collect the balance (card or cash; the card fee applies to card, chapter 18).
3. Return belongings, hand over the stay summary and the grooming result if a groom was added.
4. Press **Check out**. The room frees for the next arrival.

> NOTE: Re-opening a checked-out booking, cancelling or marking a no-show are manager moves (chapter 17).

## In-person lesson

1. Do two check-ins with the manager: one single dog, one multi-dog shared room.
2. Label belongings the house way; put meds in the meds cabinet with the room code.
3. Do one check-out and count the balance back to the parent.

## Online lesson

1. In **Today (F-01)** open an arriving booking, read pets, room and questionnaire.
2. Press **Check in**; watch the status badge change and the guest move to the *Staying* tab.
3. Open a departing booking, read the balance, press **Check out**.
