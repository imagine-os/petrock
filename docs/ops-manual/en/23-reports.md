# Reports

Reports read the same tables the desk writes to: bookings, appointments, daycare days, invoices and payments. They respect the location you are in; the owner can see all locations. Financial reports need the `reports.financial` permission (owner).

## 1. Overview (F-62)

KPIs for the chosen period: bookings, nights, grooming appointments, daycare days, revenue collected, pending vaccines, feedback waiting. A bar per day shows collected revenue; hover a bar for the value, or switch to the table view to copy the numbers.

[screenshot: F-62 — Reports overview]

## 2. Revenue (F-63)

Collected payments by day, by service (hotel / grooming / daycare) and by method (card / cash), with the card fee and tax collected shown separately. Export downloads the table as CSV.

[screenshot: F-63 — Revenue report]

## 3. Occupancy (F-64)

Nights sold against capacity per room type and location, per day; average length of stay; how full each row is today.

{{capacities}}

## 4. Reading a chart

1. One series, one colour, one axis. If two things need comparing, they get two charts.
2. The labelled bars are the highest and the latest; hover for the rest.
3. The table view is the same data; use it when you need the exact number.

## In-person lesson

Read the week with the owner once a month: what changed, what to ask the desk.

## Online lesson

1. Open **Reports (F-62)**, change the period, hover a bar, switch to the table.
2. Open **Revenue (F-63)** as the owner and export the CSV.
3. Open **Occupancy (F-64)** and compare Encino and Westwood with *all locations*.
