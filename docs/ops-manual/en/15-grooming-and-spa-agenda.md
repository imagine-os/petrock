# Grooming & Spa agenda

Grooming and Spa are one service. A groom is a **package** (Gold, Platinum, Diamond) priced by the dog's size band, plus optional **add-ons** that add minutes. The agenda is the day view (F-30): one column per groomer, appointments by time; the board (F-31) shows the same appointments by status.

{{pricing:grooming}}

{{pricing:addons}}

## 1. The day view

1. Open **Grooming › Day view** (F-30). Your column shows your appointments; the tint is your working window.
2. Duration comes from the package minutes for the size plus the add-on minutes. Do not shorten it in the system to fit more in; tell the desk.
3. Drag to reschedule inside the day; the desk confirms with the parent.

[screenshot: F-30 — Groomer columns with appointments by time]

## 2. The board

Appointments move **requested → confirmed → checked in → checked out**, the same words as hotel bookings. On the board (F-31) you drag the card to the next column when the dog is on the table and when the dog is done.

[screenshot: F-31 — Kanban board by status]

## 3. Hotel guests

Grooms for hotel guests are done at the end of the stay so the dog goes home clean. The booking says *add grooming*; the desk books the slot; you see it in your column with the room code.

## In-person lesson

1. Shadow a Gold and a Platinum groom; note which steps add minutes.
2. Learn the size bands by weighing three dogs.
3. Walk one dog from the board column "checked in" to "checked out" while the trainer watches the screen.

## Online lesson

1. Open the day view (F-30) as the groomer demo user; open one appointment.
2. Switch to the board (F-31); move one card.
3. Read the package price for a Large dog and the add-on minutes in the live tables above.
