# P-02 · Website: Hotel

## Purpose

Room types in the client's own words: the Penthouse and Suite inclusion lists from petrockhotel.com (premium bed, toys during the day, potty pads, room service, playtime, two walks a day, nightly photos and videos, bedtime tuck in and tummy rub - the Penthouse adds a TV), photos of the real rooms, the "from" nightly rate for weekday and weekend, shared-room and long-stay discounts read from the tables, and the vaccines required before a stay.

## Logic

- from = min(rates where season_id null) per room type; weekend rate shown when present.
- Room count = sum of penthouse + suite capacities across locations.
- Room photo per `room_types.key` (penthouse -> the penthouse wall, suite -> the mural corridor), falling back to the glass-front rooms.
- The large-dog note repeats the site's "Large Dog Accommodation fee may be applicable" wording without a number; the amount is an open owner question (D-229).

## Photos used

## Real vs mock

Copy and photos are the client's own, from the 2026-09-20 petrockhotel.com crawl (D-224), served as committed WebP derivatives from `public/site/img` through `SitePhoto` (D-225). Everything else reads the mock provider (localStorage) through the same `useTable` hooks the app uses; every price is computed from the pricing tables (R-X71), now seeded from the live site (D-229), never typed. Squarespace stays live at petrockhotel.com until this site replaces it. When Company-OS lands, `DataProvider` swaps and nothing on the page changes.

## Responsive check (D-016, D-194)

Checked at 360, 390, 768, 1280, 1920, 2560 and 3840 on 2026-09-20 (Playwright against `vite preview`): no horizontal page scroll at any width, body text >= 16 px at 1920+. Header nav becomes a slide-in drawer under 960 px; grids collapse to one column under 600 px; photos serve the 480 / 960 derivative on phones.
