# F-13 · Room timeline

## Purpose

Rooms as rows, days as columns, stays as blocks coloured by status (D-008: designed fresh around rooms and stays). The desk sees occupancy for one or two weeks, spots vaccine / balance / note flags on each block, drags a stay to another room or start day, and acts from the block popover (Open, Assign room, Set status). Clicking an empty cell starts a booking for that room and day.

## Rules

- `R-I09`, `R-I01` - block colour = status hue
- `R-I07` - context actions in the popover
- `R-X07` - a move keeps the nights; target must be the booked type, fit the heaviest dog (R-E09) and be free (R-X03)
- `R-I06`, `R-X05` - PIN-gated transitions; checked-in stays only change room, not dates

## Logic

- Block = [check-in day, check-out day) starting at the check-in half-day; same-day stays take one cell.
- Colour from booking status tokens (R-I09); flags: vaccine issue, balance due, notes, medication.
- Drop onto (room, day): nights kept, target room must be the booked type, fit the heaviest dog (R-E09) and be free (R-X03); confirmed in a modal, logged as a booking event (R-X07).
- Empty cell click starts a new booking for that room and day.
- Bar label is "Pet · Customer", truncated; a bar narrower than 76 px shows a "…" chip instead and keeps the full label in its aria-label and popover.
- The popover opens on click, Enter and hover; a keyboard-opened popover takes focus, so dragging is never the only way (D-195).

## Actions (D-196, D-197)

Declared in the page spec; this is what a voice / agent controller and the future WebMCP tools drive. Every UI change on this page updates the list in the same commit.

| id | Label | Intent | Permission | Params |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `reservations.switchView` | Switch view | show the reservations as a table, timeline or board | - | `view: enum:table,timeline,board` |
| `reservations.setWeek` | Change the week | move the timeline to another week | - | `start: date` |
| `reservations.setSpan` | One or two weeks | show one week or two weeks of the timeline | - | `span: enum:7,14` |
| `reservations.filter` | Filter the timeline | filter the timeline by room type, status, daycare, unassigned or cancelled rows | - | `roomType: id, status: string, showDaycare: string, showUnassigned: string, showClosed: string` |
| `reservations.openStay` | Open a stay | open the popover or detail of one stay | - | `id: id` |
| `reservations.collapseGroup` | Collapse a room group | collapse or expand a room-type group | - | `group: id` |
| `reservations.moveStay` | Move a stay | move a stay to another room or start day | `bookings.write` | `id: id, room: id, day: date` |
| `reservations.assignRoom` | Assign a room | assign or change the room of a stay | `bookings.write` | `id: id` |
| `reservations.setStatus` | Set the booking status | move a stay through the booking lifecycle | `bookings.status` | `id: id, status: string` |
| `reservations.newBookingForCell` | Book an empty cell | start a booking for that room on that day | `bookings.write` | `room: id, day: date` |

## Responsive check (D-016)

Checked at 360, 390, 768, 1280, 1920, 2560 and 3840, light and dark (2026-09-21, D-194): the grid scrolls horizontally inside its card with a sticky room column (96 px on phones) and a sticky day header; the page itself never overflows. At 2560 / 3840 the day columns and the 13 px bar label scale with the width band; the dashed today line and the "…" chip stay legible from a distance. Degrades gracefully on phones as D-016 allows.
