## Rules

- `R-X70` - only reviews with status `published` appear
- `R-X71` - every price on the page is computed from the pricing tables, never typed
- `R-K02` - open-now from `locations.hours` and the browser clock
- `R-A01` - book-now hands off into the customer app

## Logic

- Teasers = min standard-season nightly rate per room type, Gold `price_s`, full-day daycare price (R-X71).
- The hero video mounts only when `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`, the viewport is at least 700 px wide, and `navigator.webdriver` is false; the poster photo is always underneath, so phones, reduced-motion users, a blocked embed and every Playwright capture show a real photo (D-226). The play / pause button reflects and drives that state.
- Open now = current time inside `locations.hours` for today; Call buttons are `tel:` links built from `locations.phone`.
- Reviewer shown as first name + initial; the average is computed client-side from the published rows.

## Real vs mock

Copy, photos and the hero video are the client's own, from the 2026-09-20 crawl (D-224); the photos are committed WebP derivatives served from `public/site/img` (D-225) and the video is the client's YouTube background embed. Everything else reads the mock provider (localStorage) through the same `useTable` hooks the app uses, and every price comes from the pricing tables (R-X71), now seeded from the live site (D-229). Squarespace stays live at petrockhotel.com until this site replaces it. When Company-OS lands, `DataProvider` swaps and nothing on the page changes.

## Responsive check (D-016, D-194)

Checked at 360, 390, 768, 1280, 1920, 2560 and 3840 on 2026-09-20, light and dark (Playwright against `vite preview`): no horizontal page scroll at any width, body text >= 16 px at 1920+, hero type and the play / pause control scale up at >= 2560. Header nav becomes a slide-in drawer under 960 px; the hero drops to poster-only under 700 px; grids collapse to one column under 600 px.
