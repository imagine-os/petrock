## Purpose

One service in the client's own words: spa bath, blow dry, brush out, teeth and a paw massage on every package, up to the full Diamond haircut. Gold / Platinum / Diamond tiles with their inclusion lists, the full package-by-size price table, the add-ons menu ("On the side"), the $12 sanitation-fee and in-person-estimate notes, and photos of the real salon.

## Logic

- Prices per size column from packages.price_* (S/M/L/XL/Giant); minutes range minutes_s–minutes_giant.
- Add-on "from" when starting_at.
- The sanitation note reads `fees` where kind = `grooming_sanitation` and prints its amount; without the row it falls back to wording with no number (R-G06, R-X71).
- Prices are the live-site grid seeded in D-229; the XL ranges the site quotes ($120-140 Platinum, $150-175 Diamond) are seeded at their low end with the range in `packages.notes`.

## Real vs mock

Copy and photos are the client's own, from the 2026-09-20 petrockhotel.com crawl (D-224), served as committed WebP derivatives from `public/site/img` through `SitePhoto` (D-225). Everything else reads the mock provider (localStorage) through the same `useTable` hooks the app uses; every price is computed from the pricing tables (R-X71), now seeded from the live site (D-229), never typed. Squarespace stays live at petrockhotel.com until this site replaces it. When Company-OS lands, `DataProvider` swaps and nothing on the page changes.

## Responsive check (D-016, D-194)

Checked at 360, 390, 768, 1280, 1920, 2560 and 3840 on 2026-09-20 (Playwright against `vite preview`): no horizontal page scroll at any width, body text >= 16 px at 1920+. Header nav becomes a slide-in drawer under 960 px; grids collapse to one column under 600 px; photos serve the 480 / 960 derivative on phones.
