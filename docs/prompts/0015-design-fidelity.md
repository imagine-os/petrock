**Fonts (D-188).** The Figma mobile screens are Open Sans (about 70 of 85 deep text nodes), with Be Vietnam Pro on auth titles, profile / pet names and the front-desk display roles; Inter appears only in newer desk components. The app now loads `@fontsource/open-sans` 400 / 600 / 700 (body, `--font-sans`) and `@fontsource/be-vietnam-pro` 400 / 500 / 600 (`--font-display`) from `src/main.tsx`; the Google Inter link is gone; metric-compatible fallbacks (`Open Sans Fallback`, `Be Vietnam Pro Fallback`) keep layout stable before the fonts arrive. Google Fonts was rejected because @fontsource ships with the bundle and works offline through the proxy.
