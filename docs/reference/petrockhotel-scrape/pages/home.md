## Section: hotel-section

Hotel Menu
Petrock Hotel and Spa offers luxurious penthouses and suites catered to your dogs needs. Both of our accommodations are all-inclusive and include daycare, walks throughout the day, room service, one-on-one time and everyday we send videos, pictures and reports to keep you updated with your pets amazing experience with us. We are a boutique pet hotel and cater to your pets individual personal needs. Some pets like more one-on-one time with people, some prefer other animals and we find their best friends for life, and some pets need a belly rub before dinner time. Whatever your pet enjoys and loves, we provide it!
In addition we keep track of your pets health and well-being on a daily basis. We make sure that they enjoy eating their food otherwise we join them as a dinner guest and hand feed if necessary. We make sure that their urination and defecation are normal. We keep an eye out on their skin, coat, eyes and ears. We also accommodate to pets with any medical issues including senior pets, diabetic pets and pets with any medications. Your pets health and well-being is our number one priority.
