Introduction
Welcome to
Petrock Hotel & Spa

Information We Collect
We collect information to provide better services to all our users. Here’s the type of information we collect:
1. Personal Information
We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:
Register for an account on our app or website.
Schedule reservations for grooming, hotel stays, daycare, or training.
Upload vaccination records, pet photos, and other pet-related documents.
Make deposits and payments.
This personal information may include:
Name
Email address
Phone number
Mailing address
Pet information (name, breed, age, vaccination records, grooming preferences, etc.)
Payment and transaction information (for processing payments and maintaining transaction history)
2. Usage Data
We automatically collect certain information when you use our services, such as:
IP address
Browser type
Device type (mobile, desktop)
Operating system
Referring URLs
Pages visited, and time spent on the site or app
3. Location Data
With your consent, we may collect your device’s location to provide location-based services, such as finding nearby services or enhancing the user experience.
How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect to:
Manage bookings, pet profiles, and vaccination records.
Process payments and track transaction history.
Improve the functionality of our website and app.
Communicate with you about your reservations, service updates, and offers.
Provide customer support.
Personalize your user experience.
Analyze app and website usage through third-party analytics platforms.
