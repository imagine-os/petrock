| id | decision | rationale | source question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D-pending-auth-1 | Welcome screen uses the right-hand Figma variant: logo, "New to Petrock?", full-width **Create account**, "Already have an account? Sign in". | D-pending-auth-2 | Sign in and Forgot password get their own copy ("Welcome back", "Forgot your password?"); the "Create your Account / New To Petrock?" header stays only on sign-up. | D-pending-auth-4 | **OTP step is built** (D-010): 6-digit code, 10 min expiry, 5 attempts, resend after 30 s, delivered by email (SMS later). No OTP on every sign-in. | Keeps sign-in one step; reset and verification are where codes add security. | Q14, D-010 |
| D-pending-auth-5 | **Password policy**: min 8 characters, at least one letter and one number, not in a small common-password list; strength meter; no forced symbols, no expiry. | D-pending-auth-7 | Forgot password never reveals whether an email exists; the mock shows a demo hint only. | Account-enumeration protection. | new (D-018) |
| D-pending-auth-8 | Sign-up asks for the **usual Petrock location** (Encino / Westwood) -> `customers.home_location_id`, and an optional mobile number; email is the login id. | Location is a first-class concept (D-019); phone needed for pickup texts. | entities 1 / 20 |
