| D-0xx-a | A Grooming & Spa order can hold several pets, each with its own package and add-ons, at one time slot with one payment (`grooming_orders` groups the per-pet `appointments`). Pets are groomed side by side up to the location's grooming capacity, so the order's duration is the longest pet time, not the sum. | Figma "Groom another pet" and "Re-Create Spa" (Frame 1171276429/34); the front desk still sees one appointment per pet. | open questions 52, 53 |

| D-0xx-c | Customers may cancel in the app only while the order / day is `requested` or `pending_vaccines`.
